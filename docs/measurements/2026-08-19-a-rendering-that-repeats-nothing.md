# A rendering that repeats nothing answers every question exactly once · 2026-08-19

> ⭐⭐⭐ **THE PREVIOUS SESSION REPLACED AN ABSENCE-SHAPED VERDICT WITH A PRESENCE-SHAPED ONE
> BECAUSE *A READER CAN DESTROY THE EVIDENCE OF A PRESENCE BUT CANNOT MANUFACTURE IT*. That
> is true of a reader that LOSES text and false of one that INVENTS it.** In the library scan
> this repository already held — a machine reading of an English printing that returned a
> quarter of a million characters of noise — **44 of 246 689** distinct twelve-character
> fragments recur. So every fragment of it resolves **exactly once**, and a passage quoted out
> of its own noise attested a rule nobody has ever stated, in a row the instrument built
> without complaint.

⛔ The defect is one defect and it is in **eight instruments**, not one. Every guard this
repository owned asks whether a copy was **read**. None asked what a resolution in it is
**worth**.

> ⭐⭐ **The absence hole had been recorded as latent for two sessions and filed under the
> alphabet.** It is not about the alphabet. Changing the alphabet is what made it visible.

### 2.1 The floor, and what it is really made of

The floor is **0.01** at **twelve characters**. Every real copy held stands at least **6.7×**
above it; the rendering of noise **56×** below.

> ⛔ **THE LENGTH AND THE FLOOR ARE A PAIR, AND SAYING SO IS THE POINT.** Measured: at **six**
> characters the noise rendering recurs at 0.051 and *this same floor would pass it*. From
> eight to twenty characters it sits below every real copy held — by 30× at eight, 377× at
> twelve, 1 900× at twenty.

⚠ **It is fitted to the copies held** — seven renderings, one of them noise — and the
constant says so in the module. It is not a law about renderings. What is *not* fitted is the
phenomenon underneath it: language repeats and noise does not, at every length measured.

> ⭐⭐ And it is the exact complement of the correction the previous session made. That one
> found that resolving-exactly-once **rises** with length, so no length bound defends against
> chance. This one finds where recurrence lives: at **short** lengths. The same curve, read
> from the other end.

## 3. What was armed

`refuse_a_rendering_that_does_not_repeat`, at **nine resolutions across eight instruments** —
every place where a resolution is used as **evidence**:

| instrument | the resolution it leans on |
|---|---|
| an absence over the work | the positive control |
| an absence over a bounded passage | the two landmarks, and every spelling's attestation |
| an attestation outside a hand's reach | the attesting passage |
| an absence checked against a second reader | the fragment tying the readings to one edition |
| a second hand established from located passages | every such passage |
| a hand named in another copy | the naming passage — **and** the re-measured absence in the copy said *not* to name it |
| a copy that disagrees with itself | both statements |
| an alphabet checked for discrimination | every passage it must not mark |

⭐⭐ **A locus is deliberately NOT guarded, and that is recorded as a test rather than as
prose.** A locus is an **address**, not evidence: it says *these words are at this place in
this copy*, and in a rendering of noise that is still true — a reader following it finds the
words. What a resolution cannot do there is stand as evidence *for* something.

## 4. ⛔⛔⛔ The fixtures had the defect, so no test could have caught it

**Every copy built in the test file repeated nothing** — which is the property of the
rendering of noise itself. A suite whose fixtures share the defect cannot refuse it.

And the one test that certified the presence-shaped verdict was safe used a twenty-akshara
"rendering of noise" that was refused because **it could not supply the passage**. The real
thing supplies whatever it is asked for.

> ⭐⭐⭐ **A control that picks its own subject can agree with a claim for reasons unrelated
> to it.** The fixture agreed that a noise copy attests nothing, for the one reason a real
> noise copy does not have.

A fixture standing in for a book now repeats like one; the repeated line is **new** text,
because repeating a line quoted elsewhere would stop that quotation resolving.

## 5. The census, and the two things it caught in itself

The noise copy is **offered** to all nine resolutions and each must refuse it **naming the
copy** — not the alphabet, not the vocabulary, not the extent. Both halves are asserted: a
guard that only forbids cannot tell an empty subject from a clean one.

* ⛔ **One offer was refused for an unrelated reason.** A spelling sliced out of the copy was
  punctuation and digits, so the alphabet was refused for being *written in no script* — an
  earlier cause, right for that alphabet and wrong for this control. The control's own held
  condition caught it. ⇒ *An offer that never reaches the guard it is aimed at measures a
  different guard, and reports the same pass.*
* ⛔⛔ **One guard had no case measuring it at all.** Found by disarming each of the nine call
  sites in turn and re-running the suite: eight disarmings were caught and the ninth was not
  — the copy a hand is said **not** to be named in. Its guard could have been deleted in
  silence. The roster is now tied to the module's own count of guarded resolutions, so a
  guard armed without a case fails the count.

> ⚠ **And the disarming harness lied first.** Its first sweep reported 7 of 9 caught. Each
> disarmed variant differed from the last by the same number of bytes and was written inside
> the same clock second, so Python reused a **stale bytecode cache** and two runs measured a
> file that was no longer on disk. ⇒ *A measurement whose subject is the wrong file has
> measured nothing* — the true sweep, with bytecode caching off, is **9 of 9**.
