## 2. The word nobody requested

The first per-body re-emit carried readings under the plain word. Where the request could not
answer, they returned the analytical word without the nutation bit — **4**. Through `calc_ut`, no
reading in the survey, or in any other record beside it, had ever **requested** 4: every earlier
reading through that entry point carried the nutation bit.

A consumer's reader builds its table of words from what readings requested, and reads a returned word
only through that table. Put in its place beside the records it already holds:

| | before | with the first re-emit | with the repaired re-emit |
|---|---|---|---|
| the Moon, `calc_ut`, nutation-less | admitted | ⛔ **refused — an unreadable word, 4** | admitted |
| every other body, `calc_ut`, either word | uncovered | ⛔ refused — the same | **admitted**, except the mean node |
| the mean node, `calc_ut` and `calc` | refused — an echo | refused — an echo | refused — an echo |

⭐ **The refusal was right to fire, and it fired on a fact about this survey, not about the reader:**
a word the reader cannot place could name any source, and an unreadable word under a fixed condition
is refused for the body under every key. The survey was what had left it unplaceable.

⇒ **The repair**: each body is also read under the **analytical request** with the files present.
Honoured, it is under no condition — it admits nothing and refuses nothing — and it gives that word a
request. ⛔ **And the guard**: the survey writer refuses readings that return a word none of them
requested, or requested under a source its bits do not name. The section's generator holds its own
readings to the same rule.

⚠ **A lesson about the order of things.** The survey generator with the plain-word readings had
already been committed and pushed when the reader showed this; nothing it would have written had been
emitted or handed over. The measurement against the reader that will consume an output is not a
formality after the output exists — it is the last control on the output, and it found the defect the
suite could not, because the suite holds the instrument to its own rules and the reader holds it to
someone else's.

## 5. How the controls were shown to fire

Each check of the generators was planted out of the source one at a time, the suite run, and the
source restored and compared byte for byte: **23 of 23** for the per-body survey (one of which first
escaped — nothing tested that the value grid used the per-body assertion at all; a test was written
and it went red), and **30 of 30** for the section's generator and the survey's repair.

⚠ **The sweep's own reader lied twice before it was believed.** It matched a failing test's name with
a pattern that stops at a space, and the section's parametrised tests have spaces in their names — so
21 plants that had turned the suite red were reported as escapes. Then a list was compared with a
boolean, and every plant read as a disagreement. Each was caught by applying one plant by hand and
reading pytest's own summary. ⭐ *A sweep that reports "escaped" has made a claim about the suite, and
the claim is checked before the suite is changed for it.*
